Léonie Watson is showing us how we fail, as developers, to make the web accessible and how people with disabilities could benefit from AI.

She also raises her fears about the fast and uncontrolled growth of AI usage but how, in the smae time, it could compensate the lack of accessibility.

As she said, "<cite>If you want to resist AI, make accessibility part of everything you do</cite>".

A must-read for understanding the challenges faced by people with disabilities in their daily lives and why accessibility is so essential.
